Emberwing Pixel - credits
=========================

Dragon and rider animation
  Sliced from the reference sprite strip supplied by Donny with the project request
  (10 frames; 5 used). Processed by our own script: background/halo removal,
  horizontal flip, alignment on the rider, alpha-weighted downscale, shared
  40-colour palette, then hand-edited at art-pixel size so the legs are tucked back under
  the belly in flight (px-src/pipeline/legs.py), and packed into assets/sprites.png (map: assets/sprites.json
  and assets/sprites.js).

Backgrounds, obstacles, enemies, pickups, fire and clouds
  Original art generated for this game with an AI image generator, using the
  dragon strip as the style/palette reference, then cleaned up by script
  (white removed to transparency, halo trimmed, downscaled with a box filter,
  palette-reduced without dithering):
    assets/bg-far.png    Suncrest Vale sky, sun and far mountains
    assets/bg-hills.png  near hills, pines, bushes and flowers
    assets/sprites.png   gloom monsters (4 poses x 3 colours x 2 sizes,
                         from px-src/gen/monsters.jpg), 3 skygems, star, stone spire, mossy
                         boulder (2 sizes), watchtower (2 sizes), fire blast,
                         4 clouds (shrunk, softened and sky-tinted so they
                         read as background)
